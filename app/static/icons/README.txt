Service icons from the selfh.st/icons collection (https://selfh.st/icons/),
fetched once and served from here rather than a CDN, so the app keeps working
offline and doesn't tell anyone else which services you run.

Each icon comes in three files: <name>.svg is the plain mark, <name>-light.svg
is the light-coloured version (for dark backgrounds) and <name>-dark.svg the
dark-coloured one (for light backgrounds). See ServiceIcon in the frontend.

The icons are the property of their respective owners and are not affiliated
with selfh.st; the collection's own terms are at
https://github.com/selfhst/icons/blob/main/LICENSE.
