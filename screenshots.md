## Discover

New releases from Last.fm, Metacritic and Indie Is Not A Genre, grouped by week. Each one says why it's there ("You've scrobbled Nightmares on Wax before"), and you can follow or get notified straight from the list.

![Discover: new releases](screenshots/discover-new-releases.png)

## Artists

Every artist in your library: how much of their discography you own (albums, EPs, singles), and whether you follow them, get notified, or ignore them.

![Artists](screenshots/artists.png)

## Following

The artists you follow, sorted by their next release date.

![Following](screenshots/following.png)

## Missing

Releases you don't own, albums you only have part of, and Soulseek downloads in progress. Grab any of them with one click.

## Settings

### Help & Info

Library totals for each source, system details, and every background job with when it last ran and when it runs next.

![Settings: Help & Info](screenshots/settings-help-info.png)

### General

Theme, page descriptions, and the navigation order (the top tab is your home page).

![Settings: General](screenshots/settings-general.png)

### Library

How often artists are refreshed, which release types to watch, and the Get Hyped playlist.

![Settings: Library](screenshots/settings-library.png)

### Metadata

Where each piece of information comes from, in the order you choose: artist photos, bios, genre tags, similar artists, album covers, write-ups and previews.

![Settings: Metadata sources](screenshots/settings-metadata-sources.png)

![Settings: Metadata, setting up the sources](screenshots/settings-metadata-plugins.png)

![Settings: Metadata, releases and playback](screenshots/settings-metadata-playback.png)

### Discovery

The sources that feed the Discover page, how often they refresh, and challenge solvers for sites behind Cloudflare.

![Settings: Discovery](screenshots/settings-discovery.png)

![Settings: Discovery, more sources and challenge solvers](screenshots/settings-discovery-solvers.png)

### Downloads & quality

The quality profile (which formats are acceptable, best first), the download client, duplicate protection and automatic grabbing.

![Settings: Downloads & quality](screenshots/settings-downloads-quality.png)

![Settings: Automatic grabbing](screenshots/settings-automatic-grabbing.png)

### Notifications

When to announce a release, and where to send it: ntfy, Gotify, Discord or a custom webhook.

![Settings: Notifications](screenshots/settings-notifications.png)

### Keys & accounts

API keys and accounts for Last.fm and MusicBrainz, including the MusicBrainz rate limit.

![Settings: Keys & accounts](screenshots/settings-keys-accounts.png)

### Maintenance

How long scraped data is kept, clearing out space, artist artwork, the release calendar feed, and backup & restore.

![Settings: Maintenance, stored data](screenshots/settings-maintenance-stored-data.png)

![Settings: Maintenance, artwork, calendar and backups](screenshots/settings-maintenance-backup.png)
